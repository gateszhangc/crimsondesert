## Official platform list

Pearl Abyss answered the platform question directly in the March 12, 2026 **Launch FAQ**.

According to that official notice, Crimson Desert is available on:

- **PlayStation 5**
- **PlayStation 5 Pro**
- **Xbox Series X|S**
- **Xbox on PC**
- **Steam (PC and Mac)**
- **Epic Games Store**
- **Mac App Store**
- **GeForce NOW**

## What that means for search intent

Many Crimson Desert platform pages online stop at "PS5, Xbox, PC." That is not the full official answer anymore.

The more accurate platform summary now includes:

- dedicated console support
- PC storefront support
- Mac support
- cloud streaming through GeForce NOW

## Storefront and edition context

The Pearl Abyss gone-gold press release also said pre-orders were live across:

- PlayStation 5
- Xbox Series X|S
- Steam
- Apple Mac
- Epic Games Store

That press release lines up with the later launch FAQ and helps confirm the storefront picture close to release.

## Platform-specific details worth noting

The official FAQ and specs pages also clarify a few practical points:

- PC supports Xbox controllers and DualSense over USB.
- Cross-save is **not** available.
- Mac has dedicated performance guidance in the official specs notice.
- GeForce NOW was confirmed through a separate Pearl Abyss announcement in January 2026.

## The short answer

If someone asks, "What platforms is Crimson Desert on?" the official answer is broader than just console plus PC.

**Crimson Desert is available on PS5, PS5 Pro, Xbox Series X|S, Xbox on PC, Steam, Epic Games Store, Mac App Store, and GeForce NOW.**
