## The main character: Kliff

Pearl Abyss positions **Kliff** at the center of Crimson Desert.

On the official main site, Pearl Abyss says Kliff is trying to reunite the scattered survivors of the Greymanes, rebuild the group, and reclaim what was lost after a devastating ambush. The first feature overview also frames the world through Kliff's journey.

## The Greymanes

The official site and the story sections around it repeatedly use the **Greymanes** as the emotional anchor of the narrative.

Pearl Abyss specifically names companions around Kliff on the official site, including:

- Oongka
- Yann
- Naira

Those names appear in the context of the wider conflict in Pywel and the collapse of the fragile balance on the continent.

## Characters officially highlighted in the cast feature

The strongest dedicated character source is the official **Meet the Cast** notice from February 28, 2026.

That page spotlights:

- **Alec Newman** as the voice of Kliff
- **Stewart Scudamore** as the voice of Oongka
- **Rebecca Hanssen** as the voice of Damiane

This is important because it confirms which characters Pearl Abyss wanted to foreground close to launch.

## What is not officially confirmed here

Searches around Crimson Desert characters often drift into unverified roster pages. This guide intentionally stays narrow:

- It does **not** invent a full cast list.
- It does **not** assign roles to characters unless Pearl Abyss has done so.
- It does **not** turn speculation into canon.

## Best source-backed summary

If you need a short, accurate answer:

**Kliff is the protagonist, the Greymanes are the key companion group around him, and the official cast spotlight close to launch centered on Kliff, Oongka, and Damiane.**
