## The most useful official FAQ answers

The March 12, 2026 **Launch FAQ** is one of the strongest search-intent pages Pearl Abyss published before release. It answers the questions players actually ask before buying, downloading, or launching the game.

## Launch and pre-load timing

Pearl Abyss said Crimson Desert launches simultaneously worldwide on:

- March 19, 15:00 PT
- March 19, 22:00 GMT
- March 20, 7:00 KST

The same notice said pre-load opened on March 17 at the matching regional times for PlayStation Store, Xbox Store, Microsoft Store, and Steam.

## Internet requirements

This is one of the most important practical answers.

Pearl Abyss says players **need an internet connection for the initial setup and day-one patch** so the latest version can be installed. After that, the game can be played offline, but later updates still require internet access to download.

## Cross-save and controller support

The official FAQ is also explicit here:

- **Cross-save is not available** between different platforms.
- The **PC version supports Xbox controllers and DualSense over USB**.

## Languages

Pearl Abyss says the game interface and subtitles support **14 languages**, including English, French, Italian, German, Spanish, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Russian, Simplified Chinese, Spanish (Latin America), Traditional Chinese, Portuguese (Brazil), and Turkish.

The same FAQ says **full voiceover is available in English, Korean, and Simplified Chinese**.

## Editions and upgrades

The official FAQ also clarifies edition details:

- There is no dedicated upgrade option from Standard to Deluxe or Collector's Edition.
- The Deluxe Pack can be bought separately after launch if you already own the full game.
- The Ultimate Pack from the Collector's Edition is not available separately.
