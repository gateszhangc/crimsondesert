## The confirmed release date

The **official Crimson Desert release date is March 19, 2026**.

That is the date Pearl Abyss used in its official announcement pages, its later gone-gold messaging, and the March 12 launch FAQ published one week before release.

## Launch timing by region

Pearl Abyss did not frame launch as a rolling regional rollout. The launch FAQ instead gave a **simultaneous worldwide release time**:

- March 19, 15:00 PT
- March 19, 22:00 GMT
- March 20, 7:00 KST

That matters for search intent because players often look for a "release date" when what they really want is the actual unlock time.

## Timeline that official sources support

### September 24, 2025

Pearl Abyss revealed **March 19, 2026** as the date and tied the announcement to PlayStation's State of Play.

### January 21 to January 22, 2026

Pearl Abyss then announced that **Crimson Desert had gone gold**, confirming the game had cleared a major production milestone ahead of launch.

### March 12, 2026

The official FAQ added launch timing, pre-load timing, platform confirmation, supported languages, and setup requirements.
