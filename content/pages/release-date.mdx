## The confirmed release date

Crimson Desert released on **March 19, 2026**. That is the official date Pearl Abyss used across its announcement pages, gone-gold messaging, and the March 12, 2026 launch FAQ.

That is the clearest official answer on the page.

## Launch timing by region

Pearl Abyss did not frame launch as a rolling regional rollout. The launch FAQ instead gave a **simultaneous worldwide release time**:

- March 19, 15:00 PT
- March 19, 22:00 GMT
- March 20, 7:00 KST

That distinction matters because the calendar date and the actual unlock time were announced together.

## Timeline that official sources support

### September 24, 2025

Pearl Abyss revealed **March 19, 2026** as the date and tied the announcement to PlayStation's State of Play.

### January 21 to January 22, 2026

Pearl Abyss then announced that **Crimson Desert had gone gold**, confirming the game had cleared a major production milestone ahead of launch.

### March 12, 2026

The official FAQ added launch timing, pre-load timing, platform confirmation, supported languages, and setup requirements.

## What the launch confirms

The date is settled. What matters now are the details wrapped around it:

- Which platforms have the game?
- What hardware do you need?
- Does it require internet for setup?
- Which languages and controllers are supported?
